Suomi-NPP VIIRS data delivery ended on 2 November 2026; the archived 2012–2025 composites used here are unaffected, and later VNL years will come from NOAA-20 and NOAA-21.

Re-extraction from imagery needs a Google Earth Engine account: set `EE_PROJECT` to your Cloud project before running any `extract_*.py` script.

**GAUL tiles.** FAO GAUL-2015 level 0 stores Canada (10 features), the United States (5), Australia (3) and the West Bank (2) as several map tiles. Every extractor reduces each tile and combines the tile means with weights equal to tile area in EPSG:4326 (`gaul_tiles.py`); pixel counts are not used as weights because `bestEffort` can evaluate the tiles of one country at different scales (the pixel limit counts all bands of a call). The rows of those four countries in the annual, DMSP and monthly reductions come from `extract_p0_multitile.py` (same settings, per-tile results cached locally), extracted on 25 November 2026; the other countries' rows are unchanged.
